    (04) — Lips

    Filler or booster? Not the same thing.

    Most people ask for one when they actually want the other. There’s a difference between fuller lips and overfilled lips, and we’ll help you choose.

    Want size?

    Lip filler

    • Adds volume and shape
    • Lips look fuller, more defined
    • Instant, but it swells for a few days

    Want hydration and a subtle look?

    Lip booster

    • Lighter than filler
    • Hydrates, smooths fine lines, adds a natural glow
    • Your lips won’t get bigger, they just look healthier

    (05) — Body contouring

    Two treatments, one goal.

    Most fat reduction treatments miss the second step: they break down the fat but leave the skin loose. That’s why Lemon Lipo comes paired with Exilift.

    1. 01
      Lemon Lipo

      Breaks down stubborn, localized fat, area by area.

    2. 02
      Exilift

      Tightens and lifts the area after, so it finishes the job.

    Not instant. Not permanent. Not one session. Results take multiple sessions plus diet, exercise and consistency, and we check if it’s right for you before we book.
